Key reminders for this brand:
- The signature is ONE horizontal blue→magenta→red gradient (`--sf-gradient`). Use it sparingly — one moment per view (CTA pill, big stat numeral, section underline, or toggle on-state). Everything else is white + near-black ink.
- Japanese-primary copy, heavy gothic headings, faint wide-tracked English eyebrows above JP titles. No emoji.
- Actions are full pills; cards are 16px radius with soft neutral shadows; the only colored shadow is the brand glow under gradient CTAs.
- Fonts (Noto Sans JP + Figtree) and the logo PNG are SUBSTITUTIONS/crops — flag this and request the licensed assets for production.
