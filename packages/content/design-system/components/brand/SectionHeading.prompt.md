**SectionHeading** — the signature section opener. Faint uppercase English eyebrow → heavy JP title → short gradient underline.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="RESULTS & CASE STUDIES" title="実績・事例" />
<SectionHeading eyebrow="SERVICE" title="サービス内容" align="left" />
<SectionHeading title="お問い合わせ" onDark />
```

Center-aligned by default (matches the site). Set `onDark` over the dark hero/footer. Keep the eyebrow short and in English; the title carries the JP weight.
