**Card** — white content surface with a soft neutral shadow and 16px radius.

```jsx
<Card accentBar interactive>
  <h3>競技人材の育成</h3>
  <p>スポーツで培った遂行力をIT/DXスキルに転換します。</p>
</Card>
```

`elevation`: none · sm · md (default) · lg. `accentBar` adds the gradient top strip — use on feature/highlight cards. `interactive` lifts on hover for clickable cards. Keep cards on white or the page tint, never on the dark hero.
